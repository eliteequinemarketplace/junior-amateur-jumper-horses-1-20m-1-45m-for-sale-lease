Elite Equine Marketplace connects junior riders, adult amateur competitors, and developing professionals with show jumping horses for sale or lease at 1.20m through 1.45m — the most competitive range of junior and amateur jumper competition in North America and internationally. These horses represent the heart of the competitive show jumping market: horses that are proven enough to win, athletic enough to advance, and rideable enough for non-professional competitors.

The 1.20m – 1.30m Junior & Amateur Jumper Divisions

The 1.20m through 1.30m range encompasses the core of the junior and amateur jumper divisions at USEF-rated shows. At 1.20m–1.25m, riders compete in the low junior/amateur divisions — a stepping stone for horses and riders developing toward higher heights. At 1.25m–1.30m, the competition becomes significantly more serious — horses must have genuine scope, reliable carefulness, and the rideability to allow junior and amateur riders to perform at their best in speed classes and jump-offs. Horses at this level are among the most sought-after in the jumper market for families with competitive junior and amateur riders.

The 1.30m – 1.35m High Junior & Amateur Level

The 1.30m–1.35m range is where the junior and amateur jumper divisions become genuinely elite. Horses competing at this height must have real scope — the ability to clear 1.35m with room to spare — combined with competitive carefulness, bold bravery, and a tight, correct jump technique. Speed and rideability in the jump-off are critical at this level, and horses that combine scope with a natural turn of foot and a responsive, adjustable canter are the most valuable in this range.

1.40m – 1.45m Sub-Grand Prix — The Highest Junior & Amateur Level

The 1.40m–1.45m range represents the highest level of junior and amateur show jumping competition — and the doorstep to Grand Prix. Horses competing here have demonstrated their ability at serious international heights and are on the path toward 1.50m Grand Prix competition. For serious junior riders, developing professional riders, and ambitious adult competitors, a proven 1.40m–1.45m horse represents one of the most significant and exciting equestrian investments available.

High Children's Jumper Horses

Children's jumper horses compete at heights from 1.0m through 1.10m for riders typically 14 and under. The most competitive children's jumper horses qualify for the NAL Children's Jumper Final at Capital Challenge and the prestigious WIHS Children's Jumper Championship — requiring horses that are genuinely safe and rideable for young riders while possessing the scope and carefulness to win at the national championship level.
